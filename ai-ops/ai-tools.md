# 🤖 AI for DevOps & SRE

## 🌟 Introduction
AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor are revolutionizing how SREs work. They don't replace engineers; they make them 10x faster.

## 🛠️ Tooling
- **Claude AI:** Excellent for complex architectural discussions and debugging deep-dive logs.
- **ChatGPT:** Great for generating boilerplates (Terraform, YAML, Bash).
- **Cursor:** An AI-native IDE that understands your entire codebase.

## 📈 Use Cases for SREs
1. **RegEx Generation:** "Create a regex for parsing Nginx logs with these fields..."
2. **Post-Mortem Drafting:** Feed AI your incident logs to get a structured summary.
3. **Terraform Refactoring:** "Convert these 10 resources into a reusable module."
4. **Shell Scripting:** Automate complex string manipulation in seconds.

## ⚠️ The Golden Rule
**Verify, don't just trust.** Always review AI-generated code for security vulnerabilities and architectural alignment.

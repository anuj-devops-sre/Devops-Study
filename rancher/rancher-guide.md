# 🤠 Rancher: Multi-Cluster Kubernetes Management

## 🌟 Introduction
Rancher is an open-source software stack for teams adopting containers. It addresses the operational and security challenges of managing multiple Kubernetes clusters across any infrastructure.

## 🟢 Level 0: Why Rancher?
- **Unified Management:** Manage EKS, GKE, AKS, and on-prem clusters in one UI.
- **Authentication:** Centralized RBAC using AD/LDAP/GitHub.

## 🟡 Level 1: Key Features
- **Cluster Provisioning:** Deploy K3s or RKE clusters with a few clicks.
- **App Catalog:** Helm charts as "one-click" apps.
- **Monitoring & Logging:** Out-of-the-box integration with Prometheus/Grafana.

## 🟠 Level 2: SRE at Scale
- **Fleet:** GitOps at scale for thousands of clusters.
- **Security Scans:** CIS Benchmark scanning for your clusters.
- **Multi-tenancy:** Providing "Kubernetes as a Service" to your developers.
